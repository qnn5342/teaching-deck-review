# Activity design contract

## Rhythm rule

Do not pass more than three new concepts without a related demo or learner activity. Count concepts, not slides. A screenshot or trainer demonstration does not count as learner practice unless the learner has a task and output.

## Demo versus activity

- **Demo:** teacher performs; learners observe, predict, or answer a focused question.
- **Activity:** learners choose, build, classify, run, explain, or diagnose something.

An outcome that says learners can perform a task requires learner practice, not only a demo.

## Required activity fields

For every activity, specify:

```text
Concept practiced:
Learner input:
Learner action:
Learner output:
Success check:
Instructions time:
Doing time:
Debrief time:
Fallback if tool/network fails:
```

"Any questions?" and "Does this make sense?" are not activities.

## Density and timing

Count instructions, setup, doing, troubleshooting, sharing, debrief, transitions, and buffer. If the lesson is too long, reduce scope or move reference material; do not silently delete practice. If an activity cannot fit its full contract, mark it as a demo or redesign it.

## Review questions

- What does the learner actually do?
- What artifact or answer proves they did it?
- How does the teacher know whether it is correct?
- Does the activity test the stated outcome rather than recall a label?
- What happens when the demo fails?
