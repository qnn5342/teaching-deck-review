# Teaching deck review rubric

## Scorecard

| Criterion | Max |
|---|---:|
| Outcome and learner context | 15 |
| Storyline, hierarchy, and headlines | 15 |
| Clear, concrete copy | 15 |
| Activities and evidence of learning | 15 |
| Examples and demos tied to action | 10 |
| Timing and conceptual/visual density | 10 |
| Technical accuracy | 10 |
| Visual, brand, and readability | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Anchors

- 0: absent, contradictory, or unusable.
- Half credit: present but the teacher must supply substantial missing structure.
- Three-quarter credit: usable with local defects.
- Full credit: evidence is complete enough for another teacher to run it as intended.

## Evidence rules

Every non-trivial score or finding cites slide/page numbers. `N/V` means not verifiable, not zero. Do not award technical points without runtime/docs evidence or visual points without rendered review. A benchmark shows taste and teaching behavior; it does not prove technical truth.

## Priority

- **P0:** blocks safe or accurate teaching; unresolved technical claim, dangerous instruction, or broken core activity.
- **P1:** materially harms learning flow, clarity, learner practice, or classroom execution.
- **P2:** polish after the lesson works.

## Readiness gate

Recommended threshold: at least 80/100, no criterion below 60% of its maximum, no P0, verified technical claims, rendered visual review, learner output for practical outcomes, and timed rehearsal. If evidence is unavailable, report the gate as unverified.
